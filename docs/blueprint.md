# **App Name**: The Digital Da Vinci

## Core Features:

- Interactive Portfolio: A visually-driven gallery page to showcase all projects with filterable categories (Archviz, Product Design, Games, Apps, Interactive Experiences).
- Contact Form: A clean and professional contact page with a contact form and links to relevant social profiles.
- AI-Powered Project Recommendations: An AI tool analyzes user-submitted design preferences to suggest relevant portfolio projects, enhancing personalized discovery and engagement.
- Blog Section: Blog and Insights section to publish articles related to "vibe coding", tutorials and industry insights.
- About Me: A dedicated page detailing the artisan's story and background, emphasizing the "Digital Da Vinci" identity.
- Lazy Loading: Lazy loading for images and videos.
- 3D Model Background: 3D Abstract Model in the background while scrolling
- Responsiveness: Ensure website responsiveness across all modern devices.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep gold (#D4AF37) to evoke luxury and craftsmanship.
- Background color: Light grey (#F0F0F0), providing a neutral canvas.
- Accent color: Terracotta (#E2725B) to provide highlights.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', serif, to convey classic artistry.
- Body font: 'Inter', sans-serif, for clean and modern readability.
- Minimalist line icons complement the professional aesthetic, ensuring clarity without clutter.
- Clean, grid-based layout to highlight content.
- Smooth scrolling to elevate the user experience
